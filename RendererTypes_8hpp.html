Detailed Description

Declares fundamental POD types used by the renderer subsystem.

Minimal Raylib-focused variant that provides:

  • TextureHandle: opaque identifier returned by the renderer backend.
  • PixelFormat: supported pixel formats for texture creation.
  • Vec2f, Rect, RGBAColor: utility math and color helpers.
