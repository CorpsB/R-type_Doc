Detailed Description

Manages and orchestrates a stack of active scenes.

The SceneManager acts as the core control unit for the client-side scene system. Scenes are owned via unique pointers, ensuring safe and deterministic destruction order. Transitions (push, pop, replace) are deferred to prevent mid-frame modifications.

Example usage:

auto &sceneManager = rtype::client::SceneManager::getInstance();
sceneManager.pushNew<MainMenuScene>();
sceneManager.update(dt);
sceneManager.draw();

◆ flushTransitions()

void rtype::client::SceneManager::flushTransitions ( )

Applies all pending scene transitions.

Executes all queued operations in FIFO order:

  • Push: Pauses current scene (if any), activates the new one.
  • Pop: Exits current scene, resumes the previous one.
  • Replace: Destroys the current scene and activates the new one.
Note
All pending operations are cleared afterward.
