Detailed Description

Enumerations describing error categories and severities.

ErrorType enumerates logical subsystems that may produce errors. ErrorSeverity classifies how serious an error is and indicates potential runtime response. These types are intentionally coarse-grained to keep error handling simple and portable across engine subsystems.
