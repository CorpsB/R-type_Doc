enum asge::components::PLAYER_PAD : uint8_t

Enumerates eight-directional movement intentions.

The enum models an abstract directional pad commonly used by top-down or shooter games. It encodes the last dominant direction detected by the input subsystem so movement systems can translate it into velocity updates.
