8/**
9 * @file NullAudio.hpp
10 * @brief Null implementation of the IAudio interface (no-op driver).
11 * @since 0.1
12 * @ingroup audio
13 * @see IAudio, AudioManager
14 *
15 * @details
16 * The NullAudio class provides a no-operation implementation of the IAudio interface.
17 * It is primarily used by the server or headless builds of the engine, where no
18 * audio backend (such as Raylib or OpenAL) is required.
19 *
20 * All methods are implemented as safe no-ops returning default values.
21 * This ensures that any component depending on IAudio can still call its methods
22 * without conditional compilation or runtime checks.
23 *
24 * Typical use-cases:
25 * - Server-side engine instances that do not need sound.
26 * - Unit testing of components requiring an audio interface.
27 *
28 * @note The NullAudio never throws exceptions and always returns safe defaults.
29 * @warning Any call to playback methods (e.g., playSong, stopSong) will have no effect.
30 */
31
