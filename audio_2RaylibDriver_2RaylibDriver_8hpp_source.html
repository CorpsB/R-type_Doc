8/**
9 * @file RaylibDriver.hpp
10 * @brief Raylib-based implementation of the AudioDriver interface.
11 * @since 0.1
12 * @ingroup audio
13 *
14 * @details
15 * The `RaylibDriver` provides a concrete implementation of the `AudioDriver` interface
16 * using the Raylib audio backend (or an equivalent audio engine). It handles:
17 * - initialization and shutdown of the audio backend,
18 * - a cache of sound/music entries identified by logical IDs,
19 * - playback control (play/stop/loop/pitch),
20 * - global master volume management.
21 *
22 * Access to the internal entries map (`_entries`) is thread-safe via a mutex (`_mtx`)
23 * to ensure safe concurrent access between loading threads and the main thread.
24 *
25 * The cache is limited by `MAX_CACHE_SOUNDS` to avoid excessive memory usage.
26 * High-level systems (like `AudioManager`) can decide when to unload unused entries.
27 *
28 * @note This driver is designed for **client builds**.
29 * For headless or server builds, use @ref asge::audio::NullAudio instead.
30 *
31 * @see AudioDriver, AudioManager, NullAudio
32 */
33
