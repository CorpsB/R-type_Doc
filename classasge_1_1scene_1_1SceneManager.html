Detailed Description

Implements the IScene interface to manage a stack of scenes.

The SceneManager is responsible for the main game loop's scene flow. It holds a stack of scenes, but only the scene at the top of the stack (the "active" scene) is updated and drawn.

All transition operations (push, pop, replace) are deferred. They are queued as pending operations and executed only when flushTransitions() is called, typically at the end of a frame. This prevents modifying the scene stack during an update or draw call.

void asge::scene::SceneManager::flushTransitions ( )
overridevirtual

Executes all pending scene transitions (push, pop, replace).

This method iterates over the queue of pending operations and applies them to the scene stack in the order they were requested. This should be called at a safe point in the game loop, typically once per frame.
