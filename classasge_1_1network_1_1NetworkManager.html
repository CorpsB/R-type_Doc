Detailed Description

High-level network service with reliability, sessions, and dispatch.

Responsibilities:

  • Owns a NetworkChannel (transport).
  • Manages per-peer Connection objects (seq/RTT/activity).
  • Encodes/decodes NetHeader and enforces MTU/flags.
  • Reliable sending on channel 0 (one in-flight per connection).
  • Best-effort for channels > 0.
  • ACK-only handling and periodic retransmission via RTO backoff.

Lifecycle:

  1. Construct with a transport channel.
  2. startServer() or connectTo().
  3. send(), poll() in the main loop.
  4. shutdown() to terminate.
